Beatloom — deleting your data

App by kakagkai · Android · package io.github.kakagkai.beatloom

Beatloom has no account and no server. We keep nothing on our side, so there is no account for us to delete. Everything that can be removed is removed by you, directly on your device.

What exists, and who holds it

  • On your device (yours to delete) — your projects, recordings, imported audio, presets, undo history, backups and settings. These never leave your phone, and we cannot see them.
  • On Google's side (Google is an independent controller) — your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information and your interaction with an ad. Google collects this when it shows an ad you chose to watch.
  • Purchase records — held by Google Play Billing, which confirms to the app that a purchase belongs to your Google account. We never receive your name, email or card details.

How to delete it

  • Everything Beatloom stored: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Settings → Apps → Beatloom → Storage. Both remove all projects and recordings.
  • Files you exported: they are in the Documents/Beatloom folder on your device. Delete them there.
  • Advertising data: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID on your phone, and reset your ad choices through the consent form in Settings → Beatloom Pro → Ad privacy choices.
  • Anything else: write to us and we will answer within 30 days. We hold no personal data, so in practice there is nothing for us to erase.

Retention

On-device data is kept until you delete it or uninstall. We retain nothing after that. Data processed by Google for ads and purchases follows Google's own retention periods, which are described in Google's privacy policy and in your ad settings.
